# sec-procedures

Security and privacy policies and procedures for the Entropa project family: entropa.space (a signed, tamper-evident ledger for AI decisions), hippagrchealthcare.site (a synthetic-data healthcare analytics showcase) and bigironbank.online. Published openly so anyone can read what we commit to and check it against the public ledgers.

**Read this first**
- **All data is synthetic.** No real patient, customer or personal data is processed.
- **Aligned with, mapped to; not compliant, not certified.** Our policies are written against the HIPAA Security Rule, the shape of HITRUST e1/i1, the NIST AI RMF and ISO/IEC 42001. They have not been independently assessed. A certificate would be third-party evidence of a claim; our evidence is the signed, public record.
- **Honest limit.** One human operates every role; independence comes from technical gates (approvals, Cedar policy), reviewers from different model families, the signed audit trail and the human's final authority, not from organizational separation.
- Stage codes in the policies (for example S14a, Gate G1) refer to our internal build plan.

## Contents
- `policies/` P0-P12, one page each, testable MUST / MUST NOT statements, with a dated status line per mechanism.
